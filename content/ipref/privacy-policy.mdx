# Privacy Policy

## Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, iPref uses your account identifier, name, email address, and profile picture, when available, to authenticate your identity and manage your account.

Information you provide through the app may include meal details, photos, ingredients, cooking instructions, nutritional values, calendar schedules, meal consumption records, and daily calorie goals.

## How We Use Your Information

iPref uses your information to provide and maintain essential app features, including account access, meal management, scheduling, nutrition tracking, and progress monitoring.

Your meal schedules and notification preferences are also used to provide timely reminders and daily calorie updates.

## Data Storage

Your account information is managed through iPref’s authentication service. Meals, schedules, calorie goals, nutrition records, drafts, and associated photos are stored locally on this device for your signed-in account and are not synchronized to iPref’s hosted services.

The app keeps an account-specific encrypted local database. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage can permanently erase your saved data and photos. iPref Cloud for synchronization across devices is coming soon.

## Notifications and Reminders

iPref provides optional reminders for upcoming meals and notifications showing your daily calorie progress.

Meal reminders are managed on your device. Depending on your notification settings, meal details may appear in notification previews, including on your lock screen.

You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

## Third-Party Services

iPref uses Google Sign-In for secure account authentication and third-party hosting services to support application functionality.

Google processes authentication information according to its own Privacy Policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Data Protection

iPref uses account-based access controls and appropriate technical measures to help protect your information from unauthorized access.

While we take reasonable precautions to safeguard your data, no electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

## Your Choices and Control

You can create, update, and delete saved meals, manage calendar schedules, adjust reminder preferences, and sign out of your account.

You can also permanently delete your account through the application’s Settings.

## Account Deletion

Deleting your iPref account permanently removes your account and associated application data covered by the deletion process.

Once deletion is completed, the action cannot be reversed, and iPref cannot restore your account or recover deleted data.

Deleting your iPref account does not affect your Google account.

## Policy Updates

We may revise this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to iPref’s features, data practices, or applicable requirements.

The latest version will be made available through the application, with its revision date updated accordingly.
