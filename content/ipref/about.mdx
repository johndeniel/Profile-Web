# About iPref

## Made for Better Meal Planning

iPref is a meal-planning and nutrition-tracking application designed to make organizing your meals simpler, more convenient, and more intentional.

From planning what to eat to keeping track of what you've consumed, iPref brings your meals, schedules, and nutritional information together in one place.

## Plan Your Meals, Your Way

Create and save your favorite meals with ingredients, cooking instructions, and nutritional information. Schedule them whenever you like, whether you’re planning ahead or recording meals from previous days.

For meals you don’t need to save, Quick Entry lets you record them without creating a full recipe.

## Stay Organized Every Day

Keep track of your meals through an interactive calendar and daily timeline. See what’s planned, mark meals as consumed or missed, and review your meal history whenever you need it.

With Today’s Meals, your daily schedule is always within easy reach.

## Understand Your Nutrition

Monitor your calorie intake and key nutrients, including protein, carbohydrates, and fat.

Explore your nutritional progress over time, set a daily calorie goal, and stay informed about your eating habits through simple, easy-to-read insights.

## Never Lose Track of a Meal

Stay aware of upcoming meals with customizable reminders that fit your schedule.

Whether you’re preparing your next meal or checking your daily calorie progress, iPref helps keep important information close at hand.

## Our Purpose

We believe meal planning should feel simple, flexible, and accessible.

iPref is built to help you spend less time organizing what to eat and more time enjoying your meals.

Good Food for a Healthier, Happier You.

## Your data, on your device

The current version saves your meals, schedules, photos, and nutrition records on your device for your signed-in account. iPref Cloud for synchronization across devices is coming soon. Uninstalling iPref or clearing app storage can permanently erase your local data.
