# How to Delete Your Account

## Delete your account in iPref

1. Open iPref and sign in to your account.
2. Open **You**, then **Settings**.
3. Select **My account**.
4. Select **Delete Account**.
5. Read the confirmation and select **Delete permanently** to continue, or **Cancel** to go back.

## Before you confirm

This permanently removes your account, meals, Calendar entries, nutrition history and private photos. There is no recovery window.

We will verify your current sign-in before sending the request. Local drafts and photos are cleared after the server accepts the request.

Deleting your iPref account does not affect your Google account.

## If deletion does not complete

If you see **Couldn’t complete your request**, select **Try again**. If asked to wait or sign in again, follow the message before retrying.

## Request deletion without the app

Email [ipref762@gmail.com](mailto:ipref762@gmail.com?subject=iPref%20account%20deletion%20request) from the Google email address you use for iPref. Use the subject **iPref account deletion request** and ask to delete your iPref account and associated application data.

We may need to verify that the account belongs to you. Do not send your password or verification codes.

Your meals and photos are saved on your device in the current version. An email request cannot remove these local copies. To remove them, clear iPref’s app storage or uninstall it on each device where you used it. Uninstalling alone does not delete your iPref account.
