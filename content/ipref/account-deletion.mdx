# iPref Account Deletion

**Last updated: October 21, 2026**

You can permanently delete your **iPref** account and its associated application data. Deletion cannot be undone. Deleting iPref does not delete your Google account.

## Request deletion by email

You can request account deletion without installing or opening iPref.

[Email an iPref account deletion request](mailto:ipref762@gmail.com?subject=iPref%20account%20deletion%20request&body=Please%20delete%20my%20iPref%20account%20and%20its%20associated%20application%20data.%0A%0AMy%20iPref%20sign-in%20email%3A%20)

1. Email **[ipref762@gmail.com](mailto:ipref762@gmail.com)** from the Google email address you use to sign in to iPref.
2. Use the subject **iPref account deletion request** and state that you want your iPref account and associated application data deleted.
3. If you cannot access that email address, explain this in your request. We may need to verify account ownership before acting on it.

Do not send your password, Google verification codes, or meal photos. Sending an email starts a request; it does not confirm that deletion has completed. If you need an update, reply to the same email conversation.

## Delete your account in the app

1. Open iPref and sign in to the account you want to delete.
2. Open **You → Settings → My account**.
3. Choose **Delete Account**, review the confirmation, and confirm deletion.
4. If prompted, sign in again with the same Google account. Keep an internet connection available while the request completes.

After successful deletion, the app returns to the sign-in screen and removes that account’s local data. If a request fails, iPref shows a retry message and keeps local data until completion is confirmed.

## What data is deleted?

| Data                                        | What deletion covers                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| iPref account                               | The managed iPref identity, account association, and sessions covered by the deletion process. Your Google account is unaffected.                                                                         |
| Hosted application data                     | Associated meals, schedules, nutrition records, preferences, and private uploaded images, if present from prior use. The current version does not synchronize new meal data or photos to hosted services. |
| Data on the device used for in-app deletion | That account’s local database, saved meals, calendar entries, calorie goals, consumption records, drafts, cached and draft photos, and reminders are cleared after confirmed completion.                  |

## Data on other devices

The current version keeps meal data locally. An email request cannot remotely erase files on your phone, and deletion on one device cannot confirm cleanup on another device.

To remove remaining local copies, clear iPref’s app storage or uninstall it on each device where you used the account. This can permanently erase all iPref data stored by that installation, including data for other accounts. Logging out alone preserves local records for a later sign-in. Uninstalling alone does not request deletion of your hosted iPref account.

## Retention and recovery

Completed account deletion is permanent through iPref. It does not erase your Google account or information Google retains under its own policies.

Support email correspondence and provider operational records are separate from your in-app meals. For questions about these records, backup retention, or any required retention affecting your request, contact [ipref762@gmail.com](mailto:ipref762@gmail.com). This page does not promise immediate physical erasure from provider backups or logs.
