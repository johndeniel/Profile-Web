# iPref

iPref is a meal-planning and nutrition-tracking application designed to help you organize meals, manage schedules, record food consumption, and monitor nutritional progress.

The application provides tools for creating reusable meals, scheduling meals, recording quick entries, setting daily calorie goals, and receiving meal reminders.

## What you can do

- **Plan your meals, your way.** Save meals with ingredients, cooking instructions, and nutritional information, or use Quick Entry without creating a full recipe.
- **Stay organized every day.** Use Today’s Meals and the calendar to see what is planned and review what you have consumed.
- **Understand your nutrition.** Follow your calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat, and set a daily calorie goal.
- **Stay aware of upcoming meals.** Choose meal reminders and daily calorie progress notifications.
