# iPref

import { IPrefLinks } from '@/components/ipref-section';

Meal planning and nutrition tracking, together in one place. iPref helps you organize what to eat, record what you have consumed, and follow your nutritional progress.

## What can you do with iPref?

- **Save your meals.** Keep meal photos, ingredients, cooking instructions, and nutritional values ready to use again.
- **Plan your day.** Schedule meals in your calendar and see what is coming up in Today’s Meals.
- **Record what you eat.** Mark meals as consumed or use Quick Entry without creating a saved recipe.
- **Understand your nutrition.** Review calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat, and set a daily calorie goal.
- **Choose your reminders.** Enable meal reminders and daily calorie progress notifications on your device.

## Where is your data saved?

Sign in with Google to access iPref. The current version stores meals, schedules, nutrition records, drafts, and photos locally on your device for your account. Authentication and account deletion require an internet connection.

Cloud synchronization across devices is coming soon. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage can permanently erase your local data and photos.
